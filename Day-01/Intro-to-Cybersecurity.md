# Day 01 — Introduction to Cybersecurity

## 🎯 Objective
To gain a foundational understanding of cybersecurity concepts such as offensive vs defensive security, the CIA triad,
and how a SOC analyst works in monitoring and protecting systems.

## 🔹 Description
In this module, I learned the basic concepts of cybersecurity, including offensive and defensive security, the CIA triad, and how organizations protect systems and data.
I also understood the role of a SOC analyst in monitoring systems and responding to threats.

## 🔹 Offensive Security
Offensive security focuses on identifying vulnerabilities in systems by simulating attacks. The goal is to find weaknesses before real attackers can exploit them.

## 🔹 Defensive Security
Defensive security focuses on protecting systems by monitoring, detecting, and responding to potential threats.It involves analyzing logs,
identifying suspicious activities,and taking action to prevent attacks.

## 🔹 CIA Triad
- Confidentiality → Ensuring that data is only accessible to authorized users  
- Integrity → Ensuring that data is accurate and not modified without permission  
- Availability → Ensuring that systems and data are accessible when needed  

## 🔹 Role of a SOC Analyst
A SOC analyst is responsible for monitoring systems, analyzing security alerts, and detecting suspicious activities. 
They investigate potential threats, respond to incidents, and help protect an organization’s systems and data.

## 🔹 My Understanding
From this module, I understood that cybersecurity is not only about hacking but mainly about protecting systems and ensuring their safety.
Offensive security focuses on identifying and exploiting vulnerabilities so they can be fixed, while defensive security focuses on monitoring systems and preventing attacks.
As a SOC analyst, the main responsibility is to monitor systems, analyze alerts, and respond to suspicious activities to minimize potential damage.I also realized that SOC roles require strong observation and analytical thinking rather than just technical knowledge.

## 🔹 Real-Life Example
Example: A phishing email tricks a user into entering credentials. The attacker logs in, but a SOC analyst detects unusual login behavior and investigates it.
